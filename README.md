# Melo-music-streaming-platform
Melo — Music for the feeling. A mood-based music streaming web application

Melo is a web-based music discovery experience designed to feel like a quiet, personal listening room. Choose a mood, describe a feeling in your own words, or find a soundtrack for a moment or activity. Save songs with a date and note in a personal time capsule, change the listening atmosphere, and revisit your moods and listening history in an emotional timeline.
The website includes mood-based discovery, “Songs I Can’t Explain,” cinematic soundtracks, activity-based recommendations, “One Song, One Moment” with feedback, atmospheric themes, reusable time capsules, and a persistent audio player. The included playable tracks are SoundHelix demo compositions; personal saves and listening insights are stored locally in the browser.
